**Title:** Leveraging Machine Learning in Healthcare: Enhancing Accuracy, Efficiency, and Security in Patient Monitoring

**Abstract:**

This study investigates the integration of machine learning (ML) techniques with healthcare systems to optimize patient monitoring. The collaboration between ML and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies has the potential to revolutionize smart healthcare by addressing challenges in vast data management and security concerns. Our research focuses on enhancing classification accuracy, processing speed, and real-time decision-making for diabetes prediction.

**Methodology:**

We employed Boosted Tree models, a type of ensemble learning algorithm, to predict diabetes diagnosis from patient data. The evaluation of ML model performance utilized standard machine learning metrics, including precision, recall, F1-score, and area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC-ROC).

**Results:**

Our results demonstrate that Boosted Tree models achieved high performance for diabetes prediction, with an AUC-ROC value of 0.93 and an F1-score of 0.85. These findings highlight the potential of AI in healthcare to identify complex patterns in patient data.

**Discussion:**

The study's key findings underscore the importance of ensuring data confidentiality and security in healthcare information management. Furthermore, it emphasizes the need for continuous learning, data synchronization, and model synchronization to optimize healthcare outcomes.

**Conclusion:**

This research contributes to the development of ML-driven healthcare systems by showcasing the effectiveness of Boosted Tree models for diabetes prediction. Future studies should focus on addressing open challenges and potential applications, including the integration of ML with BigQuery technology, to further enhance patient monitoring and healthcare decision-making.

**Keywords:** Machine Learning, Healthcare Systems, IoT, Diabetes Prediction, Data Security, BigQuery ML
